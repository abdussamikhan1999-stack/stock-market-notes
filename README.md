# Stock Market Notes

Notes distilled from an `/smg/` (Stock Market General, 4chan-style) thread —
education, screeners/charting, options trading, market data, and calendars.
Informational only, not financial advice.

See [LINKS.md](LINKS.md) for the full raw link list.

## Two very different tracks in this thread

The thread itself draws the line explicitly with its own section headers:
**"Boglehead Investing 101"** (long-horizon, low-effort, evidence-based
investing) vs. **"Gambling"** (its own label for options trading/
speculation). Worth taking that framing at face value rather than treating
every link here as equally low-risk.

## Learning the basics

- **Investopedia** / **Khan Academy (Economics & Finance)** — the standard
  free references for terminology and core concepts; Khan Academy is more
  structured/course-like, Investopedia is better as a lookup dictionary.
- **Bogleheads wiki "Getting Started"** (couldn't fetch — 403 from this
  environment, open directly) — this is the community built around John
  Bogle's (Vanguard founder) philosophy: broad, low-cost index funds held
  long-term beat most active trading/stock-picking after fees, over time.
  If you want the boring-but-evidence-backed approach to investing rather
  than trading, this is the canonical starting point.
- **SEC EDGAR** (sec.gov/search-filings) — the actual primary-source
  database: every public company's 10-K/10-Q filings, insider ownership
  forms (3/4/5), registration statements, full-text searchable across 20+
  years. Worth going here directly rather than trusting a summary/news
  article when you actually need to verify what a company disclosed.

## Charts, screeners, and data

- **TradingView** — the standard for charting; strong community-shared
  indicator/strategy scripts on top of the charts themselves.
- **Finviz** — a screener + heatmap tool: filter the whole market by
  fundamental/technical criteria, see sector performance as a map, track
  insider trading activity, get an economic calendar — free tier is
  genuinely useful, Elite ($24.96/mo) adds real-time data and advanced
  charting.
- **PortfolioVisualizer** / **ETFdb** — backtesting a portfolio allocation
  and researching/comparing ETFs, respectively.
- **Yahoo Finance** / **Investing.com** — general free quote/news/pre-market
  data; Yahoo Finance's "trending tickers" page is a decent quick pulse-check
  on what's getting retail attention that day.

## Options ("gambling," per the thread)

- **Options Playbook** (intro) — teaches the actual foundational concepts
  before any strategy: the Greeks (the pricing-sensitivity factors that
  affect every option), volatility (a measure of *how much* price moves,
  not which direction), and market mechanics like open interest. Structured
  explicitly to build from "Rookie" to "All-Star" — i.e., don't skip to
  strategies without the fundamentals first.
- **Options Profit Calculator** / **OptionStrat** / **Optionistics** — tools
  for modeling a specific options position's payoff before putting money on
  it — genuinely useful for understanding risk/reward on paper before
  trading, regardless of how one feels about options trading generally.

## Calendars (what's happening, and when)

- **MarketWatch economic calendar** / **Earnings Whispers** — scheduled
  macro data releases and earnings dates, respectively.
- **CME's FOMC countdown** — tracks probability of Fed rate decisions
  specifically, derived from futures pricing.
- **Mortgage News Daily building permits** — a specific, fairly niche
  leading-indicator data series for housing/construction activity.

## Live/ambient market awareness

- **Market24hClock** — one clock face showing which of ~20 global exchanges
  are currently open, plus forex/commodity dashboards; notable for
  explicitly not using tracking cookies and running on a donation model
  rather than ads.
- **FinancialJuice** — a real-time news-headline ticker for market-moving
  news.
- **CNBC/Bloomberg live streams** (via newslive.com/livestreamy.net
  mirrors) — free live financial TV, routed through stream-mirror sites
  rather than the networks' own paywalled apps.
- **iran.liveuamap.com** — a live conflict/incident map for Iran; its
  inclusion in a stock thread is about geopolitical-risk monitoring
  (Iran-related conflict risk has direct oil-price/market implications),
  not really a "stock market tool" in the conventional sense.
- **PTT Stock board / disp.cc** — Taiwanese retail-investor forums,
  included for anyone following Taiwan-listed stocks or semiconductor-
  supply-chain sentiment specifically (Taiwan is central to global chip
  manufacturing).
